            Collection

              How collecting works

              Participation achievements, not identification or conservation credentials. Observations count when they are active, valid and not future-dated. Seven-day fieldbook: entries dated on seven consecutive distinct days (backfilled/imported dates count; not proof of daily uploads). Different names count exact, trimmed names, excluding Unknown. Project completion: one complete project with at least one eligible active observation. Each item unlocks only once, ever in this local store. Duplicates, repeat events, reopen/complete, delete/restore and reload cannot multiply rewards.

              Follower and verified-observation items are shown as goals but stay locked: this prototype has no real accounts or followers and no genuine verification evidence. No rewards for rare species, precise locations, transactions or collecting wildlife. Observe safely without disturbing habitats.

              Randomized digital collectibles are unique within this local prototype only, not guaranteed unique globally. No cash, trade, NFT, blockchain or financial value. Local data can be edited or erased; this is not fraud-resistant validation. Profile/rewards are not in journal backups.

                                  🗑 Trash · 0

                                  How Trash works

                                  Delete moves a record here, not out of storage. Restore within 3 days (72 hours). After that Restore is disabled; the record is permanently removed on the next successful journal change. Closing the app pauses cleanup, not the recovery clock. No background service runs. Export includes unexpired Trash; keep backups private. Project references do not count trashed observations.

                                    ObservationsProjects

                                    LOCAL DESCRIPTIVE REPORTING

                                    Projects

                                    Collect existing journal observations. This is a count of your selected records, not an AI summary, ecological inference, species verification, or evidence of trends.

                                    How projects work

                                    Membership is always chosen manually, including previous journal entries in the inclusive start/end timeframe. Site areas are drawn on the same real map as observation locations, not geographic boundaries. Hidden or legacy records without location cannot prove inclusion. Approximate regions must fit entirely inside a boundary.

                                    Project details and included records

                                    One shared column, two separate values: site is your manual project label; species is the recorded name used for species-group matching. Both are kept in the saved scope and CSV.

                                    Choose from prior observations, or Other to enter a new value in the date, species or site fields. Values are stored as text, not HTML.
                                    Site boundary · editable after creation

                                    Saving a changed boundary removes memberships that cannot prove inclusion. Journal records are never deleted. Legacy label-only site projects remain readable; draw a boundary to enforce inclusion.

                                    Every project needs a title. Start/end dates filter all groups inclusively; day groups require a start. Species uses an exact, case-sensitive recorded name or Other/manual name. New site groups require a label and drawn boundary. Changing scope removes nonmatching memberships on save, never journal records.

                                    Include existing observations

                                    Saving and completion

                                    Projects stay in this browser. Completion locks editing until reopened; it is not a frozen snapshot. Deleting a journal record changes its summaries; missing references are shown, never counted. Journal JSON backups do not include projects. CSV is a readable report, not a project restore file.
